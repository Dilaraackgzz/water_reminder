## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email Address**: Required for account creation and authentication
- **Display Name**: Optional, for personalization
- **Profile Information**: Age, weight, height, gender, and activity level (used for water goal calculations)

### 2. Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

- **Water Intake Records**: Amount and timestamp of water consumption
- **Achievement Progress**: Your progress towards unlocking achievements
- **Streak Data**: Your daily water intake streak
- **App Usage Statistics**: How you interact with the App (via Firebase Analytics)
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers

### 3. Technical Data

- **Firebase Authentication Data**: Authentication tokens and user IDs
- **Cloud Firestore Data**: Your water records, achievements, and settings
- **FCM Tokens**: For push notifications

## Data Storage and Security

### 1. Where Your Data is Stored

Your data is stored using:

- **Firebase Authentication**: Securely stores authentication credentials
- **Cloud Firestore**: Stores your water records, achievements, and profile data
- **Local Storage (Hive)**: Caches data locally on your device for offline access
- **SharedPreferences**: Stores app settings and preferences

All data is stored in Google Cloud servers (Firebase), which are compliant with industry-standard security practices.

### 2. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS
- **Authentication**: Secure Firebase Authentication with password hashing
- **Access Control**: User data is accessible only by the authenticated user
- **Firebase Security Rules**: Implemented to prevent unauthorized access

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
